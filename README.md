Objectives
Analyze customer behavior and churn patterns.
Identify key factors contributing to customer churn.
Build a predictive model to classify customers as churned or retained.
Provide actionable insights for customer retention strategies.
Dataset

The dataset contains customer information such as:

Customer demographics
Account details
Service subscriptions
Contract information
Billing and payment details
Churn status (Target Variable)

Results and Insights
Exploratory Data Analysis

Key Findings
Customers with month-to-month contracts showed higher churn rates.
Longer-tenure customers were less likely to churn.
Higher monthly charges were associated with increased churn probability.
Results

The model successfully predicted customer churn and identified important factors affecting customer retention, helping businesses make data-driven decisions.
